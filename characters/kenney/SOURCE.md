# Kenney civilian characters

Blocky Characters 2.0 by Kenney, released under CC0 1.0 Universal.
The included license allows redistribution and does not require attribution.

- [Official pack page](https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters)
- [Kenney's matching archive on OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/3d-rigged-characters)
- Runtime files: `character-a.glb`, `character-b.glb`, `character-c.glb`,
  `character-e.glb`, `character-f.glb`, `character-p.glb`, and their matching
  `Textures/texture-a.png`, `texture-b.png`, `texture-c.png`, `texture-e.png`,
  `texture-f.png` and `texture-p.png` in that same directory.
- Source animation clips used: `static` and `walk`

The models and atlases are the original files. FireFront scales, poses and
tints instances at runtime and slightly emphasizes the arm swing in memory.
