# Kenney origin details

The modular origins use two original meshes from the
[Fantasy Town Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit) (2.0).
`pillar-wood.glb` supplies the timber posts on covered porches.
`wall-wood-window-shutters.glb` supplies the shuttered window inserts.
`Textures/colormap.png` is the pack's original palette where referenced
by the glTFs. The silhouettes, roofs, walls, floors and open doorways are
FireFront geometry. Attribution is optional under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
See `License.txt` in this folder for the original notice.

The downloaded ZIP's SHA-256 is
`1a7530c09f4d2fa2cdee259876f089334f8b1f27fa86a0c4f54ef86cdd8676ef`.

The v27 Fantasy Town family uses the same pillars and shutters where
appropriate, plus `roof-gable.glb` as its main roof. The source palette
is kept on disk. Roof colors are adapted at render time.

The v27 [Mini Forest](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-forest) (1.0) family
uses `mini-forest/building-roof.glb` for the roof and
`mini-forest/building-platform.glb` for its plinth, with the original
`mini-forest/Textures/colormap.png`. Its CC0 notice is in the same
subdirectory. The downloaded Mini Forest ZIP's SHA-256 is
`8691614018075a66458e35915b8c358c2e6178648aedadafcdf313b924aa6581`.
