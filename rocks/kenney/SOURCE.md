The review also uses the existing [Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit)
copies of `rock_smallFlatB.glb`, `rock_smallC.glb`, `rock_smallA.glb` and
`rock_largeA.glb` under `public/comparison/kenney`. Their separate source
and original license records remain there. FireFront applies its stone
palette when batching the geometry at runtime. Attribution is optional
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
